About Me

I am a Master's student in Computer Engineering in the United States, specializing in perception systems, radar signal processing, and edge machine learning. My core focus lies in developing robust autonomy sensing architectures—bridging the gap between hardware-level sensor processing (such as FMCW and 4D radar) and advanced software frameworks.

Previously, I have focused on range-Doppler algorithms, MIMO beamforming, radar-camera sensor fusion, and optimizing efficient edge AI models for localized deployment environments.

Research Interests

  • Autonomy Sensing & Perception Systems
  • FMCW & 4D Radar Signal Processing
  • Radar-Camera Sensor Fusion
  • Edge Machine Learning & Embedded Systems
